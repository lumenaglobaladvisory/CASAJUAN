Our Story · Fort Lauderdale, FL

Where Every Drink Begins a Story.

Casa Juan started with a simple idea: the best part of any drink isn’t the drink itself. It’s the moment around it.

Founded in Fort Lauderdale in 2023, Casa Juan set out to bottle something harder to fake than flavor: hospitality. The kind you find at a well-run bar, where the host remembers your name, your order, and how your night is actually going. We wanted that feeling in a can, without losing what makes it real.

“People may forget the cocktail. They will never forget how Juan made them feel.”

That belief shapes everything we make. Real ingredients, no shortcuts, and a level of care most brands reserve for a physical room, not a shelf. Luxury without pretense. Hospitality without compromise. That is the promise behind Casa Juan, and it starts with our first release: a Mango Passion Fruit Vodka Martini, made the same way we would pour it for a friend.

Casa Juan is warm, not loud. Composed, not flashy. Built for people who notice the difference between something designed to impress and something designed to actually be good.

We are just getting started. Fort Lauderdale first, everywhere next.
